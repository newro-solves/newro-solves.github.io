NewRo Solves Math Circle

Non-profit project that seeks to enhance math education for families in CSDNR:

  • Features programs offering games, puzzles, and activities based on mathematics
  • Promotes exploration, logic, and reasoning that can help students understand how AI works
  • Offers FREE Math in Action! programs at New Rochelle Public Library since 2024
  • Offers activities at PTA STEM events for FREE since 2022
  • Facilitated by student volunteers from NRHS and Iona University
