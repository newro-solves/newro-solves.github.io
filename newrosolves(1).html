New Rochelle Solves

Math games and puzzles that build problem-solving skills for kids in grades K–8, without feeling like math in school.

New Rochelle Solves is a non-profit project that enhances math education for elementary and middle school students.

With Math in Action, your child learns math the way professional mathematicians believe it should be taught.
